06 / ABOUT HAZE

Built with Purpose.

More than a dashboard — HAZE is a Hyderabad-first mobility concept shaped around clarity, intelligent interaction and thoughtful product design.

THE IDEA BEHIND HAZE

Turning complex city movement into something people can understand and act on.

HAZE was designed from the ground up around one simple ambition: make urban mobility feel less fragmented. We brought live transit visualization, route intelligence, congestion awareness, environmental risk and an AI assistant into one connected experience for Hyderabad.

Every screen, interaction and visual signal was refined through iteration, testing and problem-solving — with equal attention given to functionality and the feeling of using the product. The result is a frontend prototype built to demonstrate how a smarter city mobility layer could look and work.
