ABOUT HAZE

Building a smarter city.

HAZE is Hyderabad's Advanced Zone & Express Transit intelligence platform — designed to make urban mobility simpler, faster and smarter.

HAZE brings live transit information, route intelligence, congestion insights, environmental risk and AI assistance into one connected mobility experience.

Instead of making commuters search through disconnected systems, HAZE turns complex city movement into information people can understand and act on.

From locating active vehicles to identifying congestion and future heat-exposure zones, HAZE is designed around one objective: help Hyderabad move smarter.
