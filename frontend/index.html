06 / ABOUT HAZE

Built with Purpose.

A Hyderabad-first mobility concept shaped around clarity, intelligent interaction and thoughtful product design.

THE IDEA BEHIND HAZE

Turning complex city movement into something people can understand and act on.

HAZE was created with a simple vision: make urban mobility feel less fragmented. We brought live transit visualization, route intelligence, congestion awareness, environmental risk and AI assistance into one connected experience for Hyderabad.

The project represents continuous iteration, testing, problem-solving and attention to detail. Every part of the interface was designed with the goal of making a complex mobility system feel clear, useful and futuristic without losing practicality.

HAZE is ultimately a demonstration of how a city-focused digital mobility layer could bring transportation, environmental awareness and intelligent assistance together.
